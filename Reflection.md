This is a reflection of my AI project. On a start of the project I was using a few Ai, including grok as the main.
Unfortunately I lost the conversations I had on the first lessons. I was not satisfied with the scripts chatGpt and grok gave me.
The cripts were way to simple for only Ai project, so in order to improve the scripts I used more complex Ai like claude. 
Unfortunately the conversation was not saved. But the final script I have rn, is from there. 
The thing that i saved from there is detailed explenation step by step what I had to do. That is saved in file "answers"
I used the explenation claude gave me and was asking grok for detailed explenation if i didnt understandsomething.
A simple example was that grok was giving me an explenation how to set the firebase, but I couldn't do it with instructions it gave me.
The reason was simple, when I asked another more complex AI, it told me that the information grok gave me is old.
There were new names, new layout in firebase website. So with help of another Ai, i managed to fix the issue.
This just proves that free, less complex Ai can still use old information and don't have acces to newest sources of information.
That is also an exmple of the one biggest tissues I had misinformation, the data the grok was giving me does not match what I had in my app or website.
Also one of the biggest issues was that Ai agrees with everything I say.
For example, if I ask Ai is it possible to publish a website with link, and being able to text with multiple people with script only, without using firebase and vercel.
The Ai will tell me that this is possible, and will give me false instructions. You can tell Ai that the grass is red and it will agree with you.
From what I experienced, some Ai can make pretty simple mistakes because of wrong information or other reasons, it happens in around 1 out of 10 questions.
In order to prevent it I would discuss and talk with different Ai, especially if it is about important decions in project.
For simple questions I still used grok, becuase it has almost unlimited amout of messeges and uuploads. 
But for more important and valueable decions in project I would use complex AI with usually limited amout of messeges.
For example, I used simple script that grok gave me. Asked more complex Ai with 5 messeges at max to improve my script, I gave detailed instructions of what I want to see on my website.
When it gave me the script, the main part, i sed grok to ask small questions like "where do I paste this script? or I don't like the colors, can you make them darker." 
So the main script I improved with asking less complex Ai for small changes that fit my idea.
When I was done with a script, I asked complex AI for detiled step by step explenation how to set the website.
You can see the answer in file "answers". I was following it step by step, because I was sure it is correct. 
But when I was getting stuck I was asking grok some questions like "should it be like that" or "where do I press now?".
